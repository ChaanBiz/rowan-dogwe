About Me

I’m Rowan, a 19-year-old Computer Science working student from Baguio City who likes building things that real people use.

I have always enjoyed making things, from animating Gacha Life videos for my own YouTube channel to building apps today.

While studying, I work remotely as a software engineer for Aziz Holdings in New Zealand and Elevora Technologies in the United States, and I support myself fully through this work. I am the primary mobile engineer on TaxIt, an app on the App Store and Google Play, and I build AI features for products used by real customers.

Outside work and school, I organize and host The Work Behind the Luck, a community that helps college students become problem solvers, and I make Stickomics, a YouTube channel of animated stories about tech and business. My goal is to grow from building applications to engineering dependable systems.
